# Checker Role - Quick Reference Card

## 🎯 What is Checker Role?
Read-only access to view all business data except Adjustments and POS modules.

## ✅ What Checker Users CAN Do
- ✅ View all documents (POs, Sales Orders, Invoices, etc.)
- ✅ Access all reports and export data
- ✅ View inventory levels and stock movements
- ✅ See customer and supplier information
- ✅ View financial data (Cash Flow, Expenses)
- ✅ Print documents
- ✅ Search and filter data

## ❌ What Checker Users CANNOT Do
- ❌ Create new documents
- ❌ Edit existing documents
- ❌ Delete any records
- ❌ Approve or Post documents
- ❌ Access Stock Adjustments
- ❌ Access POS module
- ❌ Change system settings
- ❌ Manage users or roles

## 📊 Modules Accessible (16 Total)

### ✅ Full Access (View Only)
1. **Dashboard** - Overview and metrics
2. **Catalog** - Items, Categories, Units
3. **Partners** - Suppliers, Customers
4. **Warehouses** - Warehouse list, Locations
5. **Procurement** - POs, GRNs, Returns, Supplier Catalog
6. **Sales** - Orders, Deliveries, Pickups, Returns, Invoices
7. **Expenses** - Expense listing and categories
8. **Supplies** - Supply items and movements
9. **Cash Flow** - Transactions and logs
10. **Services** - Customer services and invoices
11. **Inventory** - Stock levels, movements, transfers, damaged reports
12. **Pricing** - Price lists, discounts, customer catalogs
13. **QR Codes** - QR tags, scanning, printing
14. **Reports** - All reports (Sales, Expenses, Financial, Stock, etc.)
15. **Target Goals** - Business goals and targets
16. **Dictionary** - System dictionary

### ❌ No Access
- **Adjustments** (submenu under Inventory)
- **POS** (entire module)
- **Settings** (entire module)

## 🔄 How to Use

### Login
1. Go to the system URL
2. Enter your username and password
3. Click Login

### Navigation
- Use the sidebar menu on the left
- Click on any module to expand submenus
- Click on submenu items to view data

### Viewing Documents
- Click on any document number to view details
- Use filters and search to find specific records
- Click "Print" button to print documents
- Click "Back" to return to list view

### Generating Reports
1. Go to Reports module
2. Select the report type
3. Choose date range and filters
4. Click "Generate" or "Export"

## 🆘 Common Issues

### "You do not have permission to access this page"
- You're trying to access Adjustments or POS module
- These are restricted for Checker role
- Contact admin if you need access

### "Your role is view-only. You cannot make changes"
- You tried to create, edit, or delete something
- Checker role is read-only
- Contact admin if you need write access

### Can't see sidebar modules
- Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
- Log out and log back in
- Try incognito/private browsing mode
- Contact admin if issue persists

## 📞 Need Help?
Contact your system administrator if you:
- Need access to restricted modules
- Need to create or edit documents
- Experience technical issues
- Have questions about your permissions
