## ✅ Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Clear Your Browser Cache
**Windows (Chrome/Edge):**
- Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
- Select "All time" from the time range
- Check "Cached images and files"
- Click "Clear data"

**Windows (Firefox):**
- Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`
- Select "Everything" from time range
- Check "Cache"
- Click "Clear Now"

### 2. Close ALL Browser Tabs
- Close every tab and window of your browser
- Make sure the browser is completely closed

### 3. Restart Your Browser
- Open a fresh browser window
- **OR** use Incognito/Private mode:
  - Chrome: `Ctrl + Shift + N`
  - Firefox: `Ctrl + Shift + P`
  - Edge: `Ctrl + Shift + N`

### 4. Log In Again
- Go to the system URL
- Enter your username and password
- Click Login

### 5. Verify Access
Check that you can now access:
- ✅ Dashboard (should load without errors)
- ✅ Procurement module
- ✅ Sales module
- ✅ Reports module
- ✅ All other modules in sidebar
