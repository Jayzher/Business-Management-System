# Invoice Print - Quick Reference Guide

## ✅ What Was Improved

### 1. Business Logo
- **Size increased from 50px to 80px** (60% larger)
- More prominent and professional
- Better brand visibility

### 2. Font Weights
- **Company Name:** Bold (700) and larger
- **INVOICE Title:** Larger and bolder
- **Table Headers:** Bold for better readability
- **Grand Total:** Larger and bolder
- **All Labels:** Properly weighted for hierarchy

### 3. Thank You Note
- **Size increased from 11px to 14px** (27% larger)
- **Style:** Italic for elegance
- **Weight:** Medium (500) for better visibility
- **Color:** Darker for better readability

---

## 🖨️ How to Print

1. Go to **Invoices** → Select any invoice
2. Click **"Print"** button
3. Invoice will open in new tab with improved layout
4. Auto-print dialog will appear
5. Review and print
